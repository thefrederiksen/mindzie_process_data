# Partner Cheat Sheet - Hire-to-Retire

## Industry Overview

### Top Challenges in Hire-to-Retire Management
- Fragmented employee lifecycle processes causing inefficiencies and errors
- Inefficient onboarding and offboarding leading to employee dissatisfaction and compliance risks
- Difficulty in workforce forecasting and proactive staffing
- High administrative overhead due to manual HR processes
- Lack of visibility into real-time workforce metrics and performance

## How Process Intelligence Addresses These Problems

- Provides end-to-end visibility into the entire hire-to-retire lifecycle
- Identifies bottlenecks and inefficiencies through real-time process monitoring
- Automates manual tasks to significantly reduce administrative workload
- Uses AI-driven predictive analytics to forecast staffing needs and reduce overtime
- Enhances compliance with automated checks and alerts
